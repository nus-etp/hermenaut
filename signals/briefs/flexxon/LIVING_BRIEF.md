_Last updated: 2026-11-25 02:00 UTC_

## Thesis
Flexxon is a BLOCK71 Silicon Valley-listed Singapore cybersecurity-hardware startup (founded 2007) building X-PHY, the world's first AI-embedded SSD with hardware-level threat detection and physical-tamper response. Co-founded by Camellia Chan and May Chng, the company has now secured its third CyberCall funding award for an AI agent security gateway product and made its first outbound investment — into Tech4Biz Solutions to co-develop secure server infrastructure — signalling a shift from pure product development to building an ecosystem around its hardware security platform.
