## Thesis
Craft Health is an NUS GRIP-incubated Singapore health-tech startup building 3D-printed pharmaceuticals and compounding pharmacy solutions. The company has raised across two rounds (Pre-Seed in 2022 led by Mistletoe Singapore, and a Pre-Series A2 in 2024 led by Valuence Ventures) and has been developing its CraftMake 3D printing platform. The October 2026 exclusive North American distribution agreement with PharmaTher for CraftMake marks the company's first major commercial partnership for international market access, though the announcement came via LinkedIn rather than a press release or independent coverage.
