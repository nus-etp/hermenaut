_Last updated: 2026-11-28 01:17 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
Craft Health is an NUS GRIP-incubated Singapore health-tech startup building 3D-printed pharmaceuticals and compounding pharmacy solutions. The company has raised across two rounds (Pre-Seed in 2022 led by Mistletoe Singapore, and a Pre-Series A2 in 2024 led by Valuence Ventures) and has been developing its CraftMake 3D printing platform. The October 2026 exclusive North American distribution agreement with PharmaTher for CraftMake marks the company's first major commercial partnership for international market access; PharmaTher's interim MD&A dated 28 October 2026 then put terms on the record — a two-year initial term with three one-year renewals, and exclusivity that lapses unless two CraftMake systems are ordered by 31 January 2027.

## Recent signals
- **2026-10-29** — PharmaTher's interim MD&A put the previously undisclosed terms of the Craft Health distribution deal on record: a two-year initial term plus three one-year renewal options, and exclusivity that lapses to non-exclusive unless PharmaTher orders two CraftMake systems by 31 January 2027 — [sedar-filings-backup.thecse.com](https://sedar-filings-backup.thecse.com/000048941/06507477-00000001-000048941-PharmaTher_MDA_August_31_2026_final-PDF.pdf)
  - Summary: PharmaTher's interim MD&A for the quarter ended 31 August 2026, dated 28 October, describes the 1 October 2026 agreement under which it is the exclusive North American distributor of CraftMake and CraftMake Pro systems, CraftControl software licences, CraftBlends printable materials, and spare parts and consumables, plus installation, training, validation and service, covering 503A and 503B compounding pharmacies, telehealth and digital-health companies, CDMOs, pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, hospitals and health systems, and academic, research and government institutions. The term is two years, renewable at PharmaTher's option for three further one-year terms, and exclusivity depends on meeting sales targets and minimum purchase commitments, including an initial order of two CraftMake systems by 31 January 2027, after which the rights may become non-exclusive. Craft Health keeps ownership of the CraftMake, CraftControl and CraftBlends technology; PharmaTher owns the formulations, data and regulatory filings it develops on top of it and plans a first US placement at PD Labs, a 503A compounding pharmacy it calls its Center of Excellence.
  - Counterparties: PharmaTher Holdings (exclusive North American distributor), PD Labs (planned first US site, Center of Excellence)
