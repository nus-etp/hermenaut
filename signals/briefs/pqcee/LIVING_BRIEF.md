## Thesis
pQCee is a BLOCK71 Singapore-resident post-quantum cybersecurity startup (founded 2022) building scalable encryption and key-lifecycle management, with its flagship SafeQuard document-protection tool aligned to NIST post-quantum standards. Supported by the US$15M CyberSG Talent, Innovation and Growth Collaboration Centre (NUS × CSA), the company is now expanding commercially beyond Singapore: the October 2026 partnership with Carahsoft to reach the U.S. public sector and a JETRO interview outlining plans for Japan, Europe and the Middle East signal a deliberate international push. A Singapore patent in force for "Method and System for Protecting Digital Signatures" (registered 24 November 2026) underpins the product's defensible IP position.
