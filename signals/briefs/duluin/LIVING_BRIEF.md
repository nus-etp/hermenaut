_Last updated: 2026-11-28 01:17 UTC_

## Thesis
Duluin is a BLOCK71 Bandung-resident Indonesian fintech startup building an Earned Wage Access (EWA) platform plus blue-collar workforce management. Named 'Startup of the Year' at the ASEAN Startup Innovation Weekend 2026 (Phnom Penh, April 2026), the company is led by CEO Isa Nuruddin Ahmad and has announced expansion plans into Vietnam and Cambodia. The September 2026 partnership with BTN (Bank Tabungan Negara) to strengthen payroll and workforce management services marks Duluin's first named partnership with a major Indonesian state-owned bank, validating its EWA model at scale.

  - Summary: Duluin announced a partnership with BTN (Bank Tabungan Negara), an Indonesian state-owned bank, to strengthen payroll and workforce management services. The partnership integrates Duluin's Earned Wage Access platform with BTN's banking infrastructure, enabling blue-collar workers to access their earned wages through the bank's channels. This is Duluin's first publicly named partnership with a major state-owned bank, validating its EWA model at scale in the Indonesian market. Kontan's 21 September report confirms the collaboration runs nationally rather than as a pilot, and describes API-level integration of Duluin Gajian (EWA), Workin (HRMS) and Margin (accounting) with BTN's banking infrastructure for payroll disbursement, without disclosing how many employers or workers it covers.
