_Last updated: 2026-11-28 01:17 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
DANA is an Indonesian digital wallet and digital-financial-services company, founded in 2018 and built to reach users and merchants outside the country's major cities with payments, QR transactions, transfers and AI-assisted financial tools in one app. In September 2026, 01F Group's growth-stage arm 01Fintech took an undisclosed investment, with the stated intent of extending credit and capital access to MSMEs, adding micro-insurance and savings products, and building more efficient cross-border payment rails. Neither the investment size nor the resulting ownership was disclosed; DANA's own 2024 disclosures put it at about 200M accounts and 1.2M registered MSMEs, but no revenue, transaction-volume or valuation figures have been published.

_Last material event: 2026-09-22 — 01F Group, through its 01Fintech growth-stage arm, took an undisclosed investment in DANA_
